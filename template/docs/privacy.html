Glyph Template — Privacy policy

Developer: n7 · Last updated: November 15, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Glyph Template does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the internet.

What stays on your phone

The app saves only one setting on your device: whether you have already opened Glyph Toys from it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Sensors

The app reads the accelerometer while the Glyph Toy is active and while the app screen is open, only to roll the ball when you tilt the phone. This data is used on the device and never leaves it.

Vibration

The app may vibrate briefly when the ball hits the edge or a ring is fired. This needs no personal data.

Children

Glyph Template is intended for users aged 13 and over.
