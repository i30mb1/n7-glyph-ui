Glyph Template puts a little ball on the Glyph Matrix on the back of your Nothing Phone (3).

TILT IT
The ball rolls the way you tip the phone and bounces off the round edge of the matrix.

HOLD IT
Hold the Glyph button and the rim of the matrix fills up with light.

LET GO
Let go and a ring flies out of the centre and kicks the ball.

ALWAYS WITH YOU
Set Glyph Template as your Always-on Glyph Toy and the ball rests on the back of your phone.

TRY IT IN THE APP
The app shows the toy live: tilt the phone and hold the on-screen button.

Needs a Nothing Phone (3). No ads, no accounts, no data collected.

Made for the Nothing Phone (3) Glyph Matrix.
